* Hanoi campus

## Saigon South campus

#### During semester

* Monday to Friday: 8am - 9pm
* Saturday and Sunday: 8am - 5pm
* Exam Time (week 10 - 12): Monday - Sunday: 8am - 9pm

#### Semester break

Monday to Friday 8am - 5pm, closed on weekends

The library is closed on public holidays.

### Address

* Level 3, Beanland Building, RMIT Saigon South campus (702 Nguyen Van Linh boulevard, Tan Hung ward, Ho Chi Minh city, Vietnam)

### Contacts

* Email: library@rmit.edu.vn
* Phone: (+84) 28 3776 1319

### Wheelchair access

* Wheelchair access is via lifts in Building 1, the Beanland Building.
* Within the Library, Level 3 is accessible via wheelchair
* Toilets are located near the entrance to The Beanland Library.

## Hanoi campus

#### During semester

* Monday to Friday: 8am - 9pm
* Saturday and Sunday: 8am - 5pm

#### Semester break

Monday to Friday 8am - 5pm, closed on weekends

The library is closed on public holidays.

### Address

Level 2, Handi Resco Building, Hanoi campus

521 Kim Ma, Giang Vo ward, Hanoi, Vietnam

### Contacts

* Email: hanoilibrary@rmit.edu.vn
* Phone: (+84) 24 3724 5920

### Wheelchair access

Wheelchair access is via lifts from the ground floor. If you are on Level 2, contact your librarian for access.
