Students with outstanding debts
If you have an outstanding debt to the University, you will be restricted from:
•
•

enrolling in a new academic program
enrolling in a new academic year and/or semester of study

These restrictions will apply until the debt has been cleared in full.

This practice aims to minimize your potential to incur a larger debt until your current outstanding amount has been cleared.

You will remain liable for full course fees for any classes/courses dropped after the Census date. Block mode and intensive classes will have the different timeline. Please refer
to Student Fees and Charges Guide (https://www.rmit.edu.vn/study-at-rmit/tuition-fees) for more information.

Fee refunds

For more information about credit balance and refunds, please contact RMIT Student Connect.

Form submission

The effective date of submission is the date this form is received by RMIT University Vietnam.
